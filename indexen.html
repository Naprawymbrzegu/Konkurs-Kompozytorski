Composition Competition 2025

A unique artistic event in Czechowice-Dziedzice, aimed at young composers from Poland and abroad.

About the Competition

In 2025, Czechowice-Dziedzice becomes the venue for an exceptional artistic event: the composition competition "On the Right Bank of the Vistula...", aimed at young composers from Poland and abroad.

The competition's goal is not only to promote contemporary accordion music, but primarily to create a space where the new generation of creators can present their explorations, visions, and individual musical language.

It is not by chance that Czechowice-Dziedzice hosts this initiative. This is where Andrzej Krzanowski lived and created – an outstanding composer and accordionist, a charismatic figure who deeply inscribed himself in the history of Polish contemporary music. The competition takes place on the 35th anniversary of his premature death.

The results will be announced on October 28, 2025, during the "Triptych" concert in Czechowice-Dziedzice – an evening that will become a celebration of new music and a symbolic culmination of the competition's first edition.

Regulations

1. Organizers The competition is organized by:
  • Andrzej Krzanowski Foundation
  • Little Academy of Music in Czechowice-Dziedzice
  • Municipal Cultural Centre in Czechowice-Dziedzice
2. Objective The aim of the competition is to promote the work of young composers and to enrich the contemporary accordion repertoire.
3. Participants The competition is open to composers under the age of 35 (born after October 1, 1990), regardless of nationality.
4. Entry Rules Each participant may submit one original composition.
The composition:
  • must not have been previously published, performed, or awarded
  • should last between 6 and 8 minutes
  • must be written for solo accordion, optionally with electronic media
5. Submission Deadline and Procedure Deadline: October 1, 2025 (date of receipt applies)
Results announcement: October 28, 2025, during the "Triptych" concert in Czechowice-Dziedzice

Submissions must be sent electronically to:
zgloszenia@naprawymbrzeguwisly.pl

Each submission must include:
  • PDF score marked with a pseudonym
  • For compositions with electronics: PDF score + electronic part (audio files or project file)
  • Audio recording or rendering (if available)
  • Text file (form available on the website) with the author's data: name, date of birth, e-mail address, phone number, short biography – also marked with a pseudonym
  • Declaration: "I declare that I am the author of the submitted composition, which has not been performed, published, or awarded before. I accept the rules of the competition."
A prerequisite for participation in the competition is payment of an entry fee of 200 PLN to the account:

The organizers reserve the right to modify the rules (without affecting equal treatment of participants) or to cancel the competition for valid reasons.
In matters not covered by this regulation, decisions are made by the organizers.
